# Digital Growth Partner content update

Scope: 48 content pages across seven services, eight industries, eight English locations, four translated locations, five articles, nine case studies, and seven hub/profile/contact pages.

The homepage, its card copy, global navigation and visual styling remain unchanged. Privacy and terms content retain their existing operational details. Existing URLs and client result figures are preserved.

## Editorial decisions

- Keep Digital Growth Partner as the main positioning, with SEO as a delivery discipline.
- Add distinct buyer decisions, practical examples, comparison tables and relevant service links to each service, industry and English location page.
- Explain actual market coverage and remote delivery without inventing local offices or project evidence.
- Give each case study its own interpretation and FAQs. Preserve the distinction between client engagements and illustrative strategies.
- Refine all five articles, correct overconfident indexing/local-search claims, and include official references. Articles carry their actual editorial update date.
- Remove generic appended article FAQs and template-generated commercial FAQs.
- Use dedicated location and industry meta descriptions; remove repeated author branding in generated titles.

## Implementation and verification

- New page guides, case notes and article references remain editable through the CMS collections.
- Extend CMS schemas for existing content fields and preserve the established mixed-case Holistic-SEO URL.
- Preserve new article tables through the legacy Markdown editor round trip.
- Validate the production build and all 56 canonical HTML pages for unique titles/descriptions/H1s, valid internal destinations, section anchors and duplicate IDs.
- Compare homepage visible text with the production baseline before release.
- Run existing admin validation/persistence, CMS and rich-content tests, plus location-language routing checks.

Local Playwright visual checks could not run because the browser executable download failed. This does not affect the production build or HTML checks. Indexing and ranking changes require subsequent Search Console observation; this release does not claim that Google has recrawled the updated pages.
